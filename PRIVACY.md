# Privacy Policy

**Kraftli Timers**
Last updated: January 2026

## Overview

Kraftli Timers is a workout timer app that respects your privacy. We do not collect, sell, or share your personal information with third parties.

## Data Storage

**Workout Data**
The app stores your timer presets and workout history locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework.

**iCloud Sync**
If you have iCloud enabled, your data syncs across your devices (iPhone, Apple Watch) using Apple's CloudKit service. This data is stored in your personal iCloud account and is not accessible to us.

## Data We Don't Collect

- No personal information (name, email, etc.)
- No location data
- No health or fitness data beyond what you enter
- No analytics or usage tracking
- No advertising identifiers

## Your Control

You can delete your data at any time:
- **In-app**: Settings → Clear Workouts / Clear Presets / Clear All Data
- **iCloud**: Manage through iOS Settings → Apple ID → iCloud

## Third Parties

We do not share any data with third parties. The app contains no third-party SDKs, analytics, or advertising.

## Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

## Changes

We may update this policy occasionally. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date.
